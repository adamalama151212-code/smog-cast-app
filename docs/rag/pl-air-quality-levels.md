# Polish air-quality levels for particulate matter (PM10, PM2.5) – unofficial English translation

> **Unofficial translation** prepared for the smogcast knowledge base. Only the parts concerning particulate matter
> are translated. The legally binding text is the Polish original: Regulation of the Minister of the Environment of
> 24 August 2012 on the levels of certain substances in the air (consolidated text: Journal of Laws – Dziennik Ustaw –
> 2021, item 845; https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2021/845/text.pdf), as amended by the Regulation of 8 October 2019
> (Journal of Laws 2019, item 1931), in force since 11 October 2019. In case of doubt the Polish text prevails.

## Scope of the regulation (§ 1–§ 2)

The regulation sets, among others: limit values for certain substances in the air (for the protection of human
health and of plants), target values, long-term objectives, **alarm levels** – levels whose even brief exceedance may
endanger human health, **information levels**, the exposure concentration obligation, the averaging periods of
measurement results, the permitted number of exceedances and the dates by which the levels must be attained.

- Limit values for, among others, particulate matter PM2.5 and PM10 are set in Annex 1.
- Target values (including PM2.5) are set in Annex 2.
- **Alarm levels** for nitrogen dioxide, sulphur dioxide, ozone and **particulate matter PM10** are set in Annex 4.
- **Information levels** for ozone and **particulate matter PM10** are set in Annex 5.
- Levels of particulate matter are determined under actual conditions (actual temperature and pressure).
- The regulation implements, among others, Directive 2008/50/EC on ambient air quality and cleaner air for Europe.

## Annex 1 – limit values (protection of human health)

| Substance | Averaging period | Limit value | Permitted exceedances per calendar year | To be attained by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Particulate matter PM10 | 24 hours | **50 µg/m³** | **35 times** | 2005 |
| Particulate matter PM10 | calendar year | **40 µg/m³** | – | 2005 |
| Particulate matter PM2.5 | calendar year | **25 µg/m³** (phase I) | – | 1 January 2015 |
| Particulate matter PM2.5 | calendar year | **20 µg/m³** (phase II) | – | 1 January 2020 |

PM10 = concentration of dust with an aerodynamic diameter of up to 10 µm; PM2.5 = up to 2.5 µm; measured by the
gravimetric method with fraction separation or by methods recognised as equivalent.

**There is no daily (24-hour) limit value for PM2.5 in Polish law** – only annual values.

## Annex 2 – target value

| Substance | Averaging period | Target value | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particulate matter PM2.5 | calendar year | 25 µg/m³ | 2010 |

## Annex 4 – alarm levels

| Substance | Averaging period | **Alarm level** |
|---|---|---|
| Particulate matter PM10 | 24 hours | **150 µg/m³** |
| Nitrogen dioxide | 1 hour | 400 µg/m³ (value occurring for three consecutive hours over an area of at least 100 km² or a whole zone, whichever is smaller) |
| Sulphur dioxide | 1 hour | 500 µg/m³ (as above) |
| Ozone | 1 hour | 240 µg/m³ (as above) |

PM10 for alarm and information levels is measured with automatic monitors using methods equivalent to the reference
method. The PM10 alarm level of 150 µg/m³ applies since the 2019 amendment (in force from 11 October 2019).

## Annex 5 – information levels

| Substance | Averaging period | **Information level** | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particulate matter PM10 | 24 hours | **100 µg/m³** | threshold for informing the public about the risk of the PM10 alarm level being exceeded |
| Ozone | 1 hour | 180 µg/m³ | threshold for informing the public about the risk of the ozone alarm level being exceeded |

The PM10 information level of 100 µg/m³ applies since the 2019 amendment.

## Annex 6 – exposure concentration obligation

| Substance | Averaging period | Exposure concentration obligation | To be attained by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particulate matter PM2.5 | three calendar years | 20 µg/m³ | 2015 |

## Summary for PM10 (daily mean, 24 hours)

| Level | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Limit value | 50 µg/m³ | a day above it counts as an exceedance; at most 35 such days per year are allowed |
| Information level | 100 µg/m³ | the public is informed; sensitive groups are advised to limit time outdoors |
| Alarm level | 150 µg/m³ | the public is warned; everyone is advised to limit time outdoors to the necessary minimum |
