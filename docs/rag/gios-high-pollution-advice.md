> **Unofficial translation** prepared for the smogcast knowledge base. Original (Polish): Chief Inspectorate of
> Environmental Protection (Główny Inspektorat Ochrony Środowiska, GIOŚ), "Informacje o wysokich stężeniach
> zanieczyszczeń w powietrzu", https://powietrze.gios.gov.pl/pjp/content/alarm_levels (retrieved 2026-10-01; the

## Recommended behaviour at the PM10 information level (100 µg/m³)

In the event of a smog situation and an exceedance of the **information level for PM10 in the air (100 µg/m³)**, in
order to reduce exposure to high concentrations of air pollutants, **small children, pregnant women, older people
and people with heart or respiratory diseases are advised to limit their time outdoors. Adults without health
problems are advised to refrain from physical activity in the open air.**

## Recommended behaviour at the PM10 alarm level (150 µg/m³)

In the event of an exceedance of the **alarm level for PM10 (150 µg/m³)**, because of the very high concentrations of
pollutants in the air, **everyone is advised to limit their time outdoors to the necessary minimum.**

## Where exceedances are published

Notices about the risk or the occurrence of an exceedance are published in the sections "Exceedances and risk of
exceedances" of the regional (voivodeship) subpages of the Air Quality portal, in the "Regional information" module.

The tabs "Information and alarm levels" and "Limit values" publish the place, time, magnitude and cause of each
exceedance in Poland of the information level, the alarm level or the limit value whose averaging period is
1 hour or 24 hours.

## Legal basis

The substances for which alarm levels, information levels and limit values are set, and the values of these levels,
are defined by the Regulation of the Minister of the Environment of 24 August 2012 on the levels of certain
substances in the air (consolidated text: Journal of Laws 2021, item 845) and the Regulation of 8 October 2019
amending it (Journal of Laws 2019, item 1931). Among others, these regulations set:

- the alarm level for PM10, ozone (O₃), sulphur dioxide (SO₂) and nitrogen dioxide (NO₂);
- the information level for PM10 and ozone;
- the limit value with a one-hour averaging period for sulphur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide;
- the limit value with a 24-hour averaging period for sulphur dioxide and for PM10.

## How exceedances are determined

Exceedances of information levels and alarm levels are determined from measurements made with automatic monitors.
Exceedances of the limit values for sulphur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide are determined from automatic measurements;
exceedances of the limit values for PM10 are determined from both automatic and manual measurements.
